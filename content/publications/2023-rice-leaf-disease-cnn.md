summary: >-
  Compares a baseline CNN with five transfer-learning models (InceptionV3, Xception,
  ResNet50V2, NASNetLarge, VGG16) for classifying five rice leaf diseases common in
  Bangladesh. InceptionV3 performed best at 95% accuracy, against 79% for the CNN
  trained from scratch.
